05 / BOARD BRIEF
Executive technology assessment

GPT-6 Astra:
the control question

GPT-6 Astra indicates a shift from chat-based assistance towards delegated digital work. The opportunity is meaningful, but adoption should be governed by the risk of the complete system: model, tools, data, identity, permissions and human oversight.

3recommended phases
observe · constrain · scale

Strategic opportunity

Target high-friction workflows where the model can combine reasoning, coding, browser interaction and document creation. Prioritise measurable cycle-time reduction rather than general experimentation.

  • Engineering remediation
  • Research synthesis
  • Controlled back-office workflows

Principal exposure

Greater autonomy amplifies ordinary control weaknesses. Excessive permissions, weak identity boundaries and poor tool validation can turn a model error into an operational event.

Board question:
Can we reconstruct what the agent saw, decided, attempted and changed?

90-day posture

Establish a cross-functional pilot covering engineering, security, legal, procurement and data governance. Keep actions reversible and require explicit approval for privileged or external changes.

Decision criterion: scale only when value, safety and operational resilience are evidenced together.
